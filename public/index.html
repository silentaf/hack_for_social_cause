Real-time rainfall evidence, before the claim fight starts

Under India's crop insurance scheme, farmers often wait weeks for a manual field survey to even begin proving a rainfall-driven loss — and disputes over whether a real deficit occurred are common because there's no independent record of it as it happened. FasalRaksha pulls real historical and current-season rainfall for a farmer's exact plot, compares it against that plot's own multi-year norm for the same crop stage, and generates a timestamped evidence record the moment a genuine deficit shows up — so the evidence exists from day one, not after the fact.

Real data, not simulation

Live rainfall figures come from Open-Meteo's ERA5 reanalysis — genuine historical climate data for any coordinate, not a mock feed.

Honest scope

This doesn't auto-approve insurance payouts. It produces evidence and triage to speed up the human verification that still has to happen.

Built for both sides

Farmers get a plain-language advisory in their own language. Insurance/agriculture officers get a triage dashboard across every registered plot.
